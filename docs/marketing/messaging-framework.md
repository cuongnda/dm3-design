## 6. Module story

Modules are the main customer entry point.

### Approved module set
- Access Control
- Visitor Management
- Attendance
- Parking
- Video Management (VMS)
- Video Intercom

### Module story rule

Explain modules as products customers can adopt now, not as isolated mini-products with separate platform stories.

### Recommended framing

Customers usually start with one urgent workflow:
- controlling access
- handling visitors
- managing attendance from real access events
- operating parking and vehicle flow
- linking video to live events and incident review
- tying intercom actions to identity, access, and response

They can then expand on the same platform without replacing the underlying system.

### What to emphasize on module pages
- the operational job the module solves
- how it works on the shared platform foundation
- how it connects to adjacent modules
- why expansion is easier because identity, events, permissions, and audit are shared

## 7. Platform and integration stance

### Platform stance

Duall Master is **platform-based at the core, module-based in adoption**.

The platform story is credible because the product has shared:
- identity, auth, and RBAC
- tenant isolation
- device connectivity and provisioning
- realtime events and live status
- audit trail and operational history
- APIs and event flows
- cloud, on-premise, and hybrid deployment options

### Integration stance

Duall Master should be described as **integration-ready**, not closed and not “replace everything.”

Approved language:
- integrate with existing systems where that reduces switching risk
- unify the operational command layer where workflows, permissions, and audit matter most
- connect to adjacent systems such as cameras, intercoms, identity sources, HR systems, tenant apps, and reporting environments
- modernize in phases rather than force a single cutover

### Partner and delivery stance

Duall Master should be presented as **platform-owned and partner-delivered where needed**.

Approved language:
- Duall Master remains the product and platform owner
- local system integrators, deployment partners, and integration partners are a core delivery motion
- customers get one accountable platform foundation with local delivery and integration capability around it
- describe partners as qualified, experienced, or in-market unless a formal certification program is real and documented

### Avoid
- “single vendor for everything”
- “rip and replace” as the default motion
- claiming native product depth where connector/API coverage is the truth
- claiming a certified partner program unless it actually exists and is documented

## 8. AI and intelligence framing

### Approved framing

AI in Duall Master is an **assistive intelligence layer**, not the primary product category.

It should be presented as helping with:
- natural-language search across events, devices, identities, and sites
- investigation support and linked-event review
- summarization for incidents and shift handoffs
- anomaly spotting and unusual-pattern review
- guided response and recommended next steps

### Framing rules
- assistive, not autonomous
- grounded in permissioned system data
- reviewable and audit-friendly
- credible now, expandable later
- never the lead story over platform and modules

### Safe summary line

Duall Master adds assistive intelligence for search, investigation, summarization, and guided response across the same secure platform foundation.

### Avoid
- “fully autonomous security”
- “AI runs your site”
- “predicts everything”
- “agentic operations” as the lead market message

## 10. Proof points

Use proof that maps to current product truth and architecture.

### Foundation proof points
- one backend and shared data/event foundation
- module-aware console with shared UI system
- multi-tenant design with explicit tenant scoping
- realtime device and event flow over MQTT and NATS JetStream
- audit trail retained on a time-series store
- cloud, on-premise, and hybrid deployment support
- shared frontend packages and vertical-ready frontend strategy

### Value proof points
- shared identity and policy across modules
- one command surface for operators
- realtime visibility and response workflows
- governed actions with auditability
- integration support through APIs, events, and connectors
- offline-tolerant device behavior for operational resilience

### AI proof rule

Describe intelligence as a layer being built into the platform and modules. Do not imply every advanced scenario is fully shipped unless specific implementation evidence exists.

## 13. Reusable talk tracks

### 30-second intro

Duall Master is a building operations and security command platform for modern sites. It combines a secure platform foundation with modular products such as Access Control, Visitor Management, Attendance, Parking, Video Management (VMS), and Video Intercom. Across those modules, Duall Master adds an assistive intelligence layer for search, investigation, summarization, and guided response. Together, the platform and modules help customers secure sites, operate in realtime, and manage people, movement, and response from one system.

### Why now

Many sites still run security and operations through disconnected tools. Duall Master gives teams one governed, realtime system for people, movement, devices, events, and response.

### Platform explanation

The platform matters because identity, permissions, devices, events, audit, and integrations are shared underneath the modules. That is what makes expansion practical instead of messy.

### Integration explanation

Duall Master is designed to integrate with the surrounding estate, so customers can unify the workflows that matter most without replacing every system on day one.

### AI explanation

We treat AI as assistive. It helps operators search, investigate, summarize, and act faster on the same permissioned platform foundation, rather than trying to replace human judgment.

### Regional talk track

Our initial commercial focus is Vietnam and South Korea, with broader Southeast Asia expansion later. That keeps the go-to-market focused while building a stronger reference base.
