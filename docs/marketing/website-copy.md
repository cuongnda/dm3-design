**Section: Access Control**

Control who goes where — across every door, gate, turnstile, lift, and barrier in your building.

**Multi-credential flexibility.** Face recognition, fingerprint, card, PIN, QR code, and mobile BLE. Let people use what works for them — or enforce specific methods for high-security zones.

**Real-time monitoring.** Live door status, access event logs streamed to your dashboard, and instant alerts for denied access or forced entry.

**Emergency modes.** One-click fire unlock, full lockdown, or custom evacuation scenarios. When seconds matter, the system responds instantly.

**Mobile unlock.** BLE and NFC phone-as-credential support. Your phone becomes your access card — and it's the primary method for modern buildings.

**Event linking.** Every access event is automatically linked to the nearest camera footage. See who badged in — and watch the video — in one click.

**Recording management.** Monitor NVR status, storage capacity, and system health from a single dashboard.

- **Temporary access** — Time-bound access for projects or events

When a new employee joins:
1. HR system sends the record to Duall Master
2. Identity is created automatically
3. Role template assigns appropriate access
4. Credentials are issued (card, biometric, mobile)
5. Employee can enter authorized areas immediately

When they leave:
1. HR system sends termination
2. All access revoked instantly — every door, every building
3. Credentials deactivated
4. Audit log sealed

Zero manual steps. Zero security gaps.
