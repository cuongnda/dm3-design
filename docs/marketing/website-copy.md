## Positioning guardrails

Use these rules across homepage and future marketing pages:

- **Duall Master** is the external product name
- **DM3** is the internal codename and should not lead customer-facing copy
- Duall Master is a **building operations and security command platform for modern sites**
- Lead with **platform foundation + modules**
- Use **Secure / Operate / Manage** as the value story, not the primary site IA
- Present modules as the main way customers understand and adopt the product
- Keep buyer language recognizable: access control, visitor management, parking, attendance, **Video Management (VMS)**, and **Video Intercom**
- Tone: **practical, premium, flagship, security-grade, realtime, modular, integration-ready, credible**
- Avoid fluffy “building OS” language unless immediately grounded in real product capabilities
- Keep claims credible and tied to current product direction

## Section: Built as a platform. Adopted as modules.

Duall Master is not a collection of disconnected tools.

It is a shared platform foundation for identity, permissions, devices, events, audit, integrations, and realtime operations — with modular products on top.

That means customers can start with one urgent need, like Access Control or Visitor Management, then add adjacent modules without changing systems, vendors, or operating models.

### Platform foundation

One foundation for:
- identity, auth, and RBAC
- device connectivity and provisioning
- realtime events and live status
- audit trail and operational history
- tenant isolation and policy enforcement
- APIs, event flows, and integrations
- deployment flexibility across cloud, on-premise, and hybrid environments

### Modules

# 4. Messaging do / don’t

## Do

- say “Duall Master” in external copy
- say “building operations and security command platform”
- say “platform foundation + modules”
- keep module names visible
- use **Video Management (VMS)** consistently when naming the video module
- keep Video Intercom framed as a committed module, not an afterthought or experimental add-on
- make realtime and security feel concrete
- sound premium but restrained

## Don’t

- lead with pillar-only navigation
- use **DM3** as the lead product name in customer-facing pages
- overuse “all-in-one”
- claim a complete future-state stack as if already shipped
- make AI or design system the lead story
- use vague language like “transform everything” without proof
