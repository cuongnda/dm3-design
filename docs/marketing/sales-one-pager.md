## Duall Master at a glance

**Duall Master** is a building operations and security command platform for modern sites.

It combines a secure platform foundation with modular products such as Access Control, Visitor Management, Attendance, Parking, Video Management (VMS), and Video Intercom.

Across those modules, Duall Master adds an assistive intelligence layer for search, investigation, summarization, and guided response.

Together, the platform and modules help customers secure sites, operate in realtime, and manage people, movement, and response from one system.

---

## What problem it solves

Most sites still run security and operations through disconnected tools: one system for doors, another for visitors, another for parking, another for video, and too much manual coordination between them.

Duall Master brings those workflows into one governed operating environment so teams can:
- respond faster to live events
- manage people, access, and movement with less fragmentation
- keep audit, permissions, and operational visibility consistent across modules
- expand without replatforming every time a new requirement appears

## Why customers buy

### One platform, modular adoption
Start with the module that solves the immediate problem. Add adjacent modules later without changing the underlying platform.

### Realtime operations
Operators get live visibility across events, devices, people, and actions instead of relying on delayed handoffs and siloed admin tools.

### Security-grade foundation
Built with tenant isolation, RBAC, auditability, and deployment flexibility for environments where trust and control matter.

### Integration-ready
Duall Master is designed to work with surrounding systems and devices through APIs, events, and connectors, so customers can modernize in phases rather than replace everything at once.

### Platform owner, local delivery
Duall Master remains the product and platform owner, while local system integrators, deployment partners, and integration partners support implementation, rollout, and in-market execution.

## Core platform foundation

The shared foundation includes:
- identity, auth, and RBAC
- device connectivity and provisioning
- realtime events and live status
- audit trail and operational history
- APIs and integrations
- cloud, on-premise, and hybrid deployment options

This foundation is what makes the module story credible.

## Modules customers can adopt

### Access Control
Control doors, gates, turnstiles, lifts, and critical access points from one system.

### Visitor Management
Handle invitations, check-in, approvals, and visitor access with less manual coordination.

### Attendance
Turn identity and real access events into usable attendance records and workflows.

### Parking
Manage vehicles, entry points, parking sessions, and gate operations in one place.

### Video Management (VMS)
Link video operations to live events, review flows, and incident response.

### Video Intercom
Connect intercom workflows to identity, access, and operator action.
