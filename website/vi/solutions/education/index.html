Giải pháp

Safe Campuses, Smarter Schools

Modern security that's always vigilant — without turning a campus into a fortress. Protect students and staff with technology that works quietly in the background.

Campus Security

Controlled Entry

Card, face, or mobile access at every gate and building. Students, staff, and visitors each with appropriate access levels.

Sàng lọc Khách

Every visitor pre-registered or checked in. Photo captured. Badge printed. Watchlist matching for flagged individuals.

Emergency Phong tỏa

One button to lock every door. Audio announcements. Mobile alerts to all staff. Evacuation routes unlocked.

Video Surveillance

Campus-wide cameras with AI alerts for unauthorized entry, loitering, or unusual crowd behavior.

Student & Quản lý Nhân viên

  • Automated attendance — Students tap or scan at classroom entry. Absent students flagged. Parents notified.
  • Staff time tracking — Teacher and staff attendance through the same access system.
  • Multi-campus management — Manage all schools in a district from one console.
  • Parent communication — Real-time attendance notifications, emergency alerts, visitor approval.
