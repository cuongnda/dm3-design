🤖 AI / IoT Focus — Selection Guide

  • Concept A (Neural Brain) — Pure AI. Brain neural network. Says "this platform thinks."
  • Concept B (IoT Hub) — Central hub connecting devices. Perfect for IoT/connected building story. Domain colors on each device node.
  • Concept C (Smart Chip) — AI processor with 4 domain-colored circuit traces. Says "intelligent hardware platform."
  • Concept D (Smart Globe) — Global connected intelligence. Great for international/enterprise positioning.
  • Concept E (Smart Building OS) — Building + circuit board fusion. Most literal — "smart building" at a glance. Circuit traces = IoT connectivity.
  • Concept F (AI Vision Shield) — AI eye inside a security shield. Combines surveillance intelligence with protection. Very "AI security" feel.

My pick: Concept C (Smart Chip) or E (Smart Building OS) — both clearly communicate AI + IoT while being distinctive and scalable.
