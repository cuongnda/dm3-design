💡 Notes for Selection

  • Concept 1 (Shield Network) — Best for: emphasizing security + connectivity. Most "cyber" feel.
  • Concept 2 (Hex Building) — Best for: building/facility management focus. Architecture vibe.
  • Concept 3 (Connected Platform) — Best for: showing the 4 domains (SECURE/MANAGE/OPERATE/SMART) visually. Very "platform" feel.
  • Concept 4 (DM Monogram) — Best for: compact use (app icons, favicons). Premium brand feel.
  • Concept 5 (D-Shield) — Best for: bold, distinctive mark. Works great at small sizes. Monospace wordmark gives tech/developer vibe.
  • Concept 6 (Layered Stack) — Best for: representing the three domains as layers. Isometric/3D feel. Very modern.

All concepts show dark + light variants. These are SVG-based and can be refined by a designer into production-ready assets.
