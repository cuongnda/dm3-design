자주 묻는 질문

How is pricing calculated?

온프레미스: per-door licensing. 클라우드: monthly subscription per building. Contact us for a detailed quote based on your specific configuration.

Can I start with Starter and upgrade later?

Yes. All plans share the same platform. Upgrading adds modules without migration or data loss.

Is there a free trial?

클라우드 deployments include a 30-day trial. 온프레미스 deployments include a proof-of-concept period.

What hardware do I need?

Duall Master works with most access control hardware, ONVIF cameras, and SIP intercoms. We also offer our own hardware.

What about training?

All plans include onboarding. Professional and Enterprise include on-site training and implementation services.

Volume discounts?

Yes. Multi-building and multi-year contracts receive significant discounts. Contact sales.
