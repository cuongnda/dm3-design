OPERATE

Run Your Facility

Parking disputes, meeting room conflicts, broken HVAC, and energy bills. Duall Master OPERATE handles all of it — integrated with security and people data.

Parking Management

Vehicle access, space management, and payment — for cars, motorbikes, and bicycles.

License Plate Recognition

Automatic entry/exit for registered vehicles. No cards needed.

Multiple Access Methods

LPR, card, QR code, monthly pass — flexible for any parking setup.

Occupancy Tracking

Real-time space availability, guidance, and reservations.

Payment & EV Charging

Fee calculation, payment gateways, invoicing, and EV charger management.

When an employee is terminated, their parking access is revoked automatically.

Room & Resource Booking

Meeting rooms, training rooms, equipment, and amenities — bookable from anywhere.

Smart Booking

Real-time availability with Outlook and Google Calendar sync.

Access Integration

Automatically grant access for the booking period only. Book at 2pm — your card works from 1:55 to 3:05.

No-show Detection

Sensors detect empty rooms and auto-release bookings for others.

Display Integration

Room status on door-mounted displays shows real-time availability.

Maintenance & Work Orders

Keep your building running with structured maintenance workflows.

  • Work orders — Create, assign, track, and close from web or mobile
  • Preventive maintenance — Scheduled tasks with automatic generation
  • Asset registry — Equipment database with full service history
  • Vendor management — Service provider contacts, contracts, and SLAs
  • AI predictive maintenance — Predict device failures before they happen

When an IoT sensor detects a temperature anomaly, a work order is created automatically — before anyone notices.

Guard Tour & Patrol

Structured security patrols with accountability.

  • Define patrol routes with required checkpoints
  • Guards scan NFC/QR checkpoints via mobile
  • GPS and indoor location real-time tracking
  • Incident reporting during patrol with photo and video
  • Compliance reports — missed checkpoints, late patrols

IoT Sensors & Energy Management

Environmental monitoring and sustainability for smart buildings.

Environmental Sensors

Temperature, humidity, air quality (CO2, PM2.5), water leak, smoke, motion and occupancy sensors.

Energy Monitoring

Real-time power consumption per zone, floor, or tenant with cost allocation.

Smart Scheduling

HVAC and lighting automation based on occupancy data.

ESG Reporting

Sustainability metrics, carbon footprint tracking, and green building score dashboard.

The Integration Advantage

OPERATE shares data with SECURE and MANAGE for a complete picture.

  • Parking access tied to employee identity termination revokes parking
  • Room booking grants temporary access no separate key management
  • Maintenance alerts triggered by security sensors faster response
  • Guard tour integrated with CCTV verify patrols visually
  • Energy data feeds AI analytics predict and optimize
