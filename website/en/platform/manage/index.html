MANAGE

Handle All People

Employees. Visitors. Contractors. Delivery drivers. Every person who enters your building needs to be identified, authorized, tracked, and deprovisioned. One identity system for everyone.

Identity Management

One profile per person. Across every module. Across every building.

  • Unified identity — A single record for each person, shared across all modules
  • Credential management — Assign and revoke cards, biometrics, and mobile credentials
  • Role-based access — Template-based provisioning: define a role once, assign to hundreds
  • HR integration — Auto-sync from your HR system. New hire? Access provisioned. Terminated? Revoked.
  • Self-service portal — Users manage their own profiles, request access, update credentials

Visitor Management

Professional visitor handling from invitation to departure.

Before the Visit

Hosts pre-register visitors via web or mobile. Visitors receive an email or SMS with a QR code and directions.

On Arrival

Visitors check in via tablet, reception, or terminal. Photo captured. Badge printed. Host notified instantly.

During the Visit

Temporary access granted to authorized areas only. Time-limited credentials expire automatically.

After the Visit

Complete audit trail. Visit duration logged. Watchlist matching for blacklisted or VIP visitors.

Contractor Management

Temporary workers with the compliance tracking they require.

  • Register contractor companies and individual workers
  • Track safety training, certifications, and insurance validity
  • Issue time-limited credentials with zone restrictions
  • Daily check-in/out logging with time tracking
  • Block access automatically when certifications expire

Time & Attendance

Accurate attendance tracking that shares infrastructure with access control. Same readers. Same credentials. No separate hardware.

Multiple Clock-in Methods

Face, fingerprint, card, or GPS-verified mobile attendance.

Shift Management

Define schedules, rotations, and assignments for any workforce.

Overtime & Leave

Automatic calculation with approval workflows and leave system sync.

Reporting

Daily, monthly, and custom attendance reports ready for payroll.

Delivery Management

Package tracking for residential and office buildings.

  • Photo capture of every delivery
  • Instant notification to recipient via app
  • Smart parcel locker integration
  • Pickup confirmation and uncollected alerts

How It All Connects

When a new employee joins:

  1. HR system sends the record to Duall Master
  2. Identity is created automatically
  3. Role template assigns appropriate access
  4. Credentials are issued (card, biometric, mobile)
  5. Employee can enter authorized areas immediately

When they leave:

  1. HR system sends termination
  2. All access revoked instantly — every door, every building
  3. Credentials deactivated
  4. Audit log sealed

Zero manual steps. Zero security gaps.
