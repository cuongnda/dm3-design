SECURE

Protect Your Building

Every entry point. Every camera. Every alarm. Every emergency response. Unified in one security platform that gives you complete control and real-time awareness.

Access Control

Control who goes where — across every door, gate, turnstile, lift, and barrier in your building.

Multi-credential Flexibility

Face recognition, fingerprint, card, PIN, QR code, and mobile BLE. Let people use what works for them — or enforce specific methods for high-security zones.

Intelligent Access Rules

Time-based schedules, zone-based restrictions, anti-passback, interlocking, and custom policies. Define exactly who can access what, when, and how.

Zero-Latency Local Decisions

Every device makes access decisions locally against a synced person database — in under 50ms. No server round-trip. No internet dependency.

Real-time Monitoring

Live door status, access event logs streamed to your dashboard, and instant alerts for denied access or forced entry.

Emergency Modes

One-click fire unlock, full lockdown, or custom evacuation scenarios. When seconds matter, the system responds instantly.

Mobile Unlock

BLE and NFC phone-as-credential support. Your phone becomes your access card — the primary method for modern buildings.

Video Surveillance (CCTV)

Unified video management for every camera in your building — regardless of brand.

Multi-brand Support

Hikvision, Dahua, Hanwha, and any ONVIF-compatible camera. No vendor lock-in.

Live View & Playback

Multi-camera grid, PTZ control, digital zoom, timeline-based playback, and event-based smart search.

Event Linking

Every access event is automatically linked to the nearest camera footage. See who badged in — and watch the video — in one click.

Recording Management

Monitor NVR status, storage capacity, and system health from a single dashboard.

AI Threat Detection

Intelligent video analytics that turn your cameras into proactive security sensors.

  • Intrusion detection — Perimeter breach, restricted area entry
  • Behavior analysis — Loitering, fighting, running, falling
  • Object detection — Abandoned bags, missing objects, vehicles
  • People counting — Crowd detection, queue monitoring
  • Recognition — Face matching, license plate, uniform detection
  • Anomaly detection — Unusual access patterns, off-hours activity, tailgating

AI doesn't replace your guards — it makes them superhuman. They get alerts for what matters, not noise.

Intrusion Detection

Perimeter protection and alarm system integration.

  • Arm/disarm zones manually or on schedule
  • Motion sensors, door contacts, glass break, vibration detectors
  • Automatic response — alert guards, trigger recording, initiate lockdown
  • Fence sensors, beam detectors, thermal cameras for perimeter security

Intercom & Communications

Video intercom that connects entrance to occupant — anywhere.

Door Stations

Video, unlock, PIN pad, and card reader — all in one device.

Indoor Monitors

Answer calls and view cameras from wall-mounted displays.

Guard Stations

Multi-call handling and building-wide announcements.

Mobile App

Answer from anywhere, remote unlock, call history.
