Smart Security for Modern Offices

Seamless entry for employees, professional visitor handling, efficient room management, and intelligent security — all without adding complexity.

The Office Challenge

  • Employees expect badge-free, phone-based access
  • Visitors need a professional, fast check-in experience
  • Meeting rooms sit empty while people complain there's nowhere to meet
  • Attendance tracking is manual, inaccurate, or non-existent
  • Security cameras exist but nobody reviews the footage
  • Five different vendors, five different logins, zero integration

How Duall Master Solves It

Seamless Employee Access

Employees unlock doors with their phones via BLE. Access provisioned from HR automatically. Join access on day one. Leave revoked instantly.

Professional Visitor Management

Visitors get a QR code, scan at the lobby, get a badge, and the host is notified. All in under 60 seconds.

Smart Room Booking

Book from Outlook or Google Calendar. Rooms auto-release when empty. Access granted only for the booking period.

Integrated Attendance

Same access readers double as attendance terminals. No separate hardware. Shift management and overtime included.

Unified Video Surveillance

All cameras in one view. Access events linked to video. AI detects loitering, tailgating, and after-hours activity.

Building Intelligence

Real-time dashboard. Occupancy analytics. Energy monitoring. AI assistant for ad-hoc queries.

A Day in the Life

8:30am

Sarah (Employee)

Walks to the turnstile. Phone unlocks it via BLE. Clocked in automatically. Meeting room booked for 9am.

9:15am

James (Visitor)

Scans QR code at lobby tablet. Takes a photo, gets a badge. Sarah gets notified. James has Floor 3 access until 11am.

2:00pm

David (Security)

Checks dashboard on phone. Camera flagged a loitering alert in parking. Taps through to live video — false alarm. Dismissed in two taps.

6:00pm

Building Auto

Low occupancy detected on Floors 4-5. HVAC reduces output. Lights dim. Energy saved without lifting a finger.
