Solutions

Secure Healthcare, Protect Patients

Hospitals must tightly control access to sensitive areas while maintaining a welcoming visitor experience. Duall Master balances openness with security.

Healthcare-Specific Security

Department-level Access

Define access policies per department. Pharmacy staff access pharmacy. Lab staff access labs. No cross-contamination.

Pharmacy Security

Multi-factor authentication for drug storage areas. Complete audit trail of who accessed what, when, and why.

Infant Security

Specialized access control for neonatal and maternity wards. Alert on unauthorized movement. Door interlocking.

Operating Theater

Restrict access to authorized surgical staff only. Track who enters and when. Integrate with scheduling.

Patient Visitor Management

  • Pre-registration by patients or staff
  • Check-in with ID verification and photo
  • Time-limited access to specific wards only
  • Visiting hours enforcement via access rules
  • VIP and watchlist management
  • Complete visitor log for compliance

Compliance & Safety

  • Immutable audit trails for regulatory compliance
  • Emergency lockdown and evacuation capabilities
  • Air quality and temperature monitoring for sensitive areas
  • Maintenance tracking for medical equipment facilities
