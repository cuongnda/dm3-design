Solutions

Smart Living for Modern Residences

Residents expect convenience. Property managers need control. Security guards need simplicity. Duall Master delivers all three.

The Resident App — Everything in Their Pocket

Digital Keys

Residents unlock lobby doors, lifts, and their floor with their phone. BLE and QR-based. Lost card? Doesn't matter.

Video Intercom

See who's at the door and unlock remotely — even from the office. Missed calls go to the app.

Visitor Invitations

Invite visitors from the app. They get a QR code. Guards verify with a scan. No phone calls, no manual logs.

Package Notifications

Guard logs delivery with a photo. Resident gets a push notification. Pickup confirmed.

Parking

Monthly passes, LPR-based entry, spot availability, EV charging status — all in the app.

Maintenance

Submit requests with photo. Track status. Get updates when resolved.

For Property Managers

  • Central dashboard — all buildings, all units, all residents
  • Visitor analytics — volume, patterns, compliance
  • Parking management — allocation, violations, revenue
  • Maintenance tracking — work orders, vendor management, resolution time
  • Resident communication — announcements, notifications
  • Revenue tracking — parking fees, facility charges, invoicing

For Security Guards

  • Simple mobile interface — guards don't need training, just a phone
  • Visitor verification — scan QR, confirm identity, grant access
  • Patrol management — NFC checkpoint scanning, incident reporting
  • AI alerts — notified about unusual activity, not every motion trigger
