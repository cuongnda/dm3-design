Why Choose Us

Why Duall Master?

Replace 5+ Systems With One

Stop juggling separate vendors for access control, CCTV, visitor management, parking, and attendance. One platform. One vendor. One login.

Mobile-First Operations

Guards, managers, and residents run their buildings from their phones. Mobile isn't an afterthought — it's the primary interface.

AI-Powered, Privacy-First

On-premise AI assistant that understands natural language. Anomaly detection that spots threats before they escalate. All your data stays in your building.

Open Architecture

Works with your existing hardware — Hikvision, Dahua, Hanwha, and any ONVIF-compatible device. Integrates with HR, ERP, and Active Directory.
