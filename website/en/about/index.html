About Us

Building Technology for the Next Generation

Duali started with a simple observation: buildings are getting smarter, but the software managing them hasn't kept up.

Security systems don't talk to facility management. Visitor logs are disconnected from access control. Energy data lives in a separate silo from occupancy data. Building managers juggle five different vendors and still can't answer basic questions about their own buildings.

We're changing that.

Our Mission

To create the operating system for buildings — a single platform where security, people management, and facility operations work together intelligently.

What We Believe

Buildings should be unified

One platform beats five point solutions. Every time.

Software should lead

We're hardware-agnostic. Your building, your choice of devices. Our software brings them together.

AI should be private

Building data is sensitive. Our AI runs on-premise. Your data stays yours.

Mid-market deserves enterprise

You shouldn't need a Fortune 500 budget to get world-class building management.

Mobile is the interface

Guards, managers, and residents live on their phones. We build for that reality.

Open architecture wins

No lock-in. Standard protocols. Your hardware, your choice.

Based in Vietnam. Built for the World.

Duali is headquartered in Vietnam, where rapid urbanization creates intense demand for modern building technology. We build for the global market — with support in English and Vietnamese, and pricing that makes enterprise-grade technology accessible.
